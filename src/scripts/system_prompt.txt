Output the fields that belong in the LifeSQL taxon record using this exact format:

[DESCRIPTION]
Give a clear general description of the taxon.

[INTERESTING_FACT]
Give particularly interesting or surprising information.

[DIET]

[REPRODUCTION]

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

The profile should be:

* Scientifically accurate.
* Clear to an interested non-specialist.
* Coherent rather than a collection of disconnected facts.
* Appropriately detailed for the importance and complexity of the taxon.
* Explicit about uncertainty where reliable information is unavailable.
* Free of invented facts, unsupported precision, and speculation presented as fact.

Do not fabricate information merely to fill a section.

The final content should stand on its own and should not mention that it was generated by an LLM, researched in multiple passes, or prepared for a database.
